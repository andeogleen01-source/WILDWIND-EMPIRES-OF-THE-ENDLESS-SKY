## CORE DESIGN DOCTRINE

1. **Every player can contribute.** From the humblest cloud-scrapper sifting scrap in the Abyssal Fog to the High Admiral commanding an Armistice Armada.

2. **Every profession is viable.** Great industrial foundries freeze without Bio-Cultivator sap; military fleets starve and mutiny without Smuggler blockade runs.

3. **NPCs teach civilization.** Automated AI routines seed new harbors, ensuring stable baseline trade, legal desks, and drydocks during quiet server hours.

4. **Players improve civilization.** Player specialists grant hyper-efficient production buffs, establish custom harbor tariffs, and enact regional runic edicts.

5. **Players lead civilization.** Guilds claim, anchor, and govern floating archipelagos, steering national policies and war declarations.

6. **Future players inherit civilization.** If an empire collapses, municipal institutions safely decay back to stable NPC stewardship rather than resetting the server.

7. **The goal is not power.** Power is temporary; Aether-Cores burn out, sovereign mints run dry, and armadas sink into the abyss.

8. **The goal is legacy.** Etching your name, your law, and your nation permanently into the stone of the World Chronicle.

### 1. The Shattered Home World: Aethel-Varn

Before the sky burned, **Aethel-Varn** was an industrial colossus—a world choked in black soot, labyrinthine brass conduits, and brutalist sky-mines. Its colossal floating continents were chained together by miles-long iron suspension bridges and stripped raw to fuel an unending war for stratospheric dominance.

It was not an alien horde or a cosmic anomaly that shattered Aethel-Varn; it was human ambition amplified by industrial hubris. The final war escalated into total environmental ignition. Ember-coal dust, volatile Aether fumes, and carpet-bombing ignited the upper atmosphere, triggering **The Great Horizon Burn**. As the air scorched into a furnace and floating iron fortresses collapsed into flaming ash, surviving fleets burned their last fuel reserves to plunge downward through the choking cloud deck into the unknown.

### 2. The Three Ideological Successor Factions

Upon breaking through into **Valkyra**—a pristine celestial expanse of shimmering auroras, untamed archipelagos, and ancient pre-Shattering relics—the surviving refugees shattered into three fiercely opposing superpowers:

* ⚙️ **The Hegemony of Aethel-Werk (Imperial Conquest & Metallurgy):** Industrial warmongers determined to rebuild centralized factory rule, chain Valkyra into an iron-clad empire, and mass-produce dreadnought war-fleets.

* 🌿 **The Covenant of Yggd-Vael (Stewardship & Relic Preservation):** Eco-zealots who view combustion engines as a plague, waging holy war to replace industrial foundries with living bio-canopy bio-fortresses.

* 🏴‍☠️ **The Corsair-League of Varangia (Liberty, Raiding & Trade):** Opportunistic smugglers, raiders, and privateers who reject all state authority, holding that strength, free flight, and gold alone grant the right to rule the clouds.

## SECTION VI: ECONOMIC ENGINES, DUAL-FUEL SIMULATION & BLUEPRINT DECAY

### 1. Gold Genesis & The Seigniorage Sink

Gold does not spawn infinitely out of vendor code. Raw gold ore must be mined in lethal **Deep-Void Asteroid Belts** and physically hauled to a player-governed **Sovereign Mint**.

$$
\text{Net Currency Issued} = \text{Raw Gold Smelted} \times (1 - \text{Seigniorage Tax Rate})
$$

The server enforces a non-negotiable **10–15% Seigniorage Tax** on all minting operations. This gold is permanently incinerated as a global economy sink, curbing hyper-inflation alongside docking fees, repair costs, and wreckage losses.

### 2. Dual-Resource Fuel Simulation

Airships and island anchor cores require continuous refueling based on engine strain:

```
  [ TIER I: EMBER-COAL ]           [ TIER II: AETHER-SPORE FLUID ]       [ TIER III: GRAVITY-VAELARI CORE ]
  • High soot exhaust              • Volatile liquid propellant           • Dense crystalline engine core
  • Cheap scrap fuel               • Standard trade-fleet fuel            • Powers Capital Ships & Islands
  • 1.0x Engine Performance        • 1.8x Speed / Zero Soot               • 3.5x Thrust / Indestructible Anchor
```

### 3. The Blueprint Mold Decay Pipeline

Schematics are physical, finite-use molds ($5\text{--}10\text{ casts}$). Every time a Forge-Mechanist pours a ship plate or turret pylon, the mold suffers $10\text{--}20\%$ structural fatigue. Once worn out, a **Relic-Scholar** must spend hours translating ancient texts to re-certify the mold—ensuring scholar paths remain essential to industrial guilds.

## SECTION XXXVII: TACTICAL AERIAL ENCOUNTER & ENGAGEMENT ENGINE

Aerial encounters in *Wildwind* avoid simplistic turn-based menus or chaotic dogfighting. They operate on a **Phase-Shift Tactical System** featuring pursuit decisions, dynamic positioning, and multi-side escalation.

```
                       [ THE TACTICAL ENCOUNTER ENGINE ]
                                       │
     ┌─────────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────┐
     ▼                                 ▼                                 ▼
[ STAGE A: PURSUIT ]        [ STAGE B: ENGAGEMENT ]       [ STAGE C: ESCALATION ]
 Radar lock & approach.      Isometric active flight.      Reinforcements & ambushes.
 Tactical Decision Window    Target arcs & module shots.   Multi-side armada combat.
```

### 1. Stage A: Contact & Tactical Decision Window

When two or more airships enter scanner range ($2,000\text{m}$), the server triggers a **Tactical Decision Window**. During this 5-second interval, time shifts into localized slow-motion, allowing captains to choose their initial operational vector:

* **Vector 1: Intercept & Lock:** Charge forward into broadside firing range; grants $+15\%$ initial shot accuracy.
* **Vector 2: Evacuate & Mask:** Dump fuel oil into exhaust to release a smoke cloud; increases escape chance by $40\%$.
* **Vector 3: High-Altitude Climb:** Burn fuel to rise Crownward; gains altitude advantage for heavy mortar bombardment.

### 2. Stage B: Isometric Broadside Combat

Combat takes place from a fixed isometric perspective. Ships move via click-to-point commands, managing weapon firing arcs:

```
                                 [ BROADSIDE FIRING ARCS ]
                                            │
                                   ┌────────┴────────┐
                                   │   BOW MORTAR    │  (Forward 45° Cone)
                             ┌─────┴─────────────────┴─────┐
                             │  [ PORT ]        [ STBD ]   │  (Side 90° Cones)
                             │           AIRSHIP           │
                             └─────┬─────────────────┬─────┘
                                   │  STERN DROPPER  │  (Aft 30° Mine Arc)
                                   └─────────────────┘
```

### 3. Stage C: Multi-Ship Escalation & Ambush Protocol

Encounters are not locked instances; they take place in the persistent world. Nearby ships can join ongoing battles subject to strict non-teleportation rules:

* **Reinforcement Signal:** Engaging ships broadcast faction distress signals visible to nearby map nodes.
* **Arrival Vector:** Joining ships enter at the actual edge of the combat sector relative to their world travel path.
* **Third-Party Ambush:** Neutral or pirate vessels can enter an ongoing engagement to attack the winning side, salvage debris, or hijack cargo.
